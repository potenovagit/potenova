# WP-00 — Specification Conflicts & Gaps Register

Findings from validating Baseline v2.0. **Severity**: High (blocks or risks a LOCKED invariant /
gate), Medium (traceability or planning risk), Low (hygiene). **Type**: Contradiction, Gap,
Ambiguity, Risk, or Decision (an IMPLEMENTATION DECISION, _not_ a spec defect).

needs to change to proceed — there are zero material deviations to approve.** The items below
are clarifications, traceability gaps, and risks to resolve at their owners/gates.

specification defects: cloud/region, framework/language, primary database & event store, event
transport, AI provider(s), voice/vision/OCR provider, identity provider, observability stack,
backup/DR mechanism, billing-reconciliation approach. Proposed stances are in the
